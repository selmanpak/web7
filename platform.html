Each asset moves through a controlled, auditable journey — with institutional checks at every stage.

Custody & security

An unbroken
chain of custody.

Every token maps to a real, custodied asset.

The connection between a token and its underlying asset is never broken — it is maintained continuously across wallets, the blockchain and official records, so ownership is always verifiable.

The platform supports multiple institutional custody models and is designed to avoid a single point of failure, giving issuers and investors flexibility without compromising security.
