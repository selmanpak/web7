Who we are

Building the rails for the future of asset ownership with AI.

FirstData is a Riyadh-based company that combines artificial intelligence with regulated digital-asset infrastructure to transform how real-world assets are owned, traded and managed.

Why we exist
Our mission

Unlock the world's real-world assets.

To transform global investment in real-world assets through AI-enabled tokenization — unlocking liquidity, transparency and access, and channeling investment into the Kingdom in alignment with Vision 2030.

Our vision

The future, illuminated.

A future where premium asset classes are accessible, liquid and transparent for a broader base of investors — locally and globally — supported by infrastructure built for trust, not speculation.

Our story

Where AI meets
asset tokenization.

Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Riyadh, FirstData was established to bring together two of the most transformative forces in modern finance: artificial intelligence and the tokenization of real-world assets.

We build the blockchain infrastructure, proprietary AI applications and advisory capability needed to move real-world assets on-chain — securely, compliantly and at institutional scale. The result is a single platform that gives asset owners new ways to raise capital, and investors new ways to access premium assets.

Everything we build is designed around one principle: trustworthy infrastructure that regulators, institutions and investors can rely on.
