Cookie Notice

Last updated: 10 June 2026

This notice explains what cookies and similar technologies are used on 1stdata.to and what choices you have. We keep our use of cookies minimal by design.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include browser local storage and session storage.

2. What cookies does this website use?

Strictly necessary

This website does not set any first-party cookies of its own. No login sessions, preferences or tracking identifiers are stored by FirstData on your device.

Third-party requests

When you visit this website, your browser makes requests to the following third-party services, which may set their own cookies or store data:

  • Google Fonts — used to load the Montserrat typeface. Google may log the request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages — the hosting infrastructure serving this site. GitHub may collect standard server log data. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Web3Forms — used only when you submit the contact form. No cookies are set by Web3Forms on page load. See Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, social media tracking pixels or any behavioural profiling technologies.

3. Managing cookies

Because this website sets no first-party cookies, there is nothing to opt out of on our side. To control cookies set by third-party services, you can:

  • Use your browser's built-in settings to block or delete cookies — all major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) include these controls under Settings → Privacy
  • Use a browser extension or DNS-based content blocker to prevent requests to Google Fonts or other third-party domains

Please note that blocking certain requests (such as Google Fonts) may affect how the website is displayed, but will not affect its core functionality.

4. Changes to this notice

If we introduce new technologies that use cookies or local storage, we will update this notice before doing so. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
